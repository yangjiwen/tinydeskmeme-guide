## 2. The Cognitive Psychology of Contrastive Satire

The core engine driving the virality of the Tiny Desk parody format is **contrastive incongruity**. 

When a viewer sees an image of a stoic Chief Financial Officer, a weary software architect, or an everyday household cat suddenly animated into a passionately emotive soul singer delivering high-register vocal riffs, the brain encounters an instant comedic surprise.

This incongruity operates across three distinct psychological dimensions:

1. **Status Deflation**: High-status figures (executives, founders, strict managers) are playfully humanized, creating irresistible relatability.
2. **Emotional Exaggeration**: Mundane daily struggles (Q3 revenue targets, unreviewed pull requests, delayed sprint deadlines) are performed with grand operatic tragedy.
3. **Earnest Micro-Cinematics**: The parody works precisely because the musical performance is deeply committed and soulful, rather than mocking or cynical.

This combination triggers strong dopamine responses, prompting viewers to tag coworkers and share the clip into internal Slack channels and WhatsApp groups.
